# Minera — User Guide

A friendly walkthrough for everyday users. No technical knowledge needed.

## What is Minera?

Minera turns your computer into an income stream. Thousands of machines team up to run one big AI "super-brain." That brain discovers genuinely new, checkable ideas — a drug pathway, a battery design, an algorithm. Those discoveries get sold, and **everyone who helped gets paid automatically**. A slice of every sale is destroyed ("burned"), which makes the MINE token scarcer over time.

You don't need a powerful computer or any crypto knowledge to start.

## Getting started (under 60 seconds)

1. Open the app.
2. Click **Sign in with Google**. A wallet is created for you automatically — no setup, no gas fees.
3. The setup wizard suggests roles for your device. Click **Start earning**.

That's it. You're mining.

## The tabs, one by one

- **Dash** — your balance, your active roles, a daily faucet button (free MINE once a day), your badges, and recent discoveries.
- **Explore** — type a question, send it to the swarm, and submit the answer as an "insight." If it's new and valid, you earn 100 MINE plus future royalties.
- **Market** — verified discoveries available to license. Licensing pays everyone who helped (you can also bet YES/NO on whether an insight will sell).
- **Bonds** — bounties: someone posts a reward for a specific breakthrough. Mine toward it, or post your own.
- **Data** — upload a dataset. If it helps shape a discovery, you earn royalties for years.
- **Roles** — all 10 ways to earn. Turn roles on/off based on what you have (GPU, just time, or just a small stake).
- **Stake** — lock some MINE to earn ~18% yield over time. Unstake whenever you like.
- **Govern** — vote on proposals that change how the network works. Your vote weight = your balance.
- **Subnets** — launch a specialized mini-network (Medical, Legal, CodeGen) and keep 20–30% of its revenue.
- **Map** — a live picture of the swarm; red dots are active miners.
- **Treasury** — full transparency: total supply, how much has been burned, and where burns come from.
- **Stats** — charts of insights, burns, and license revenue over time.
- **Board** — leaderboard of top earners.
- **Activity** — a live feed of everything happening across the network.
- **Wallet** — your full transaction history, with filters and CSV/JSON export.
- **Profile** — set a display name, bio, and avatar.
- **News / FAQ** — what's new, and plain-English answers to common questions.
- **API** — generate an API key to use the AI from your own apps (OpenAI-compatible).
- **Cash out** — convert MINE to dollars and withdraw to your bank.

## The 10 ways to earn (pick any)

| Role | Needs | How it pays |
|------|-------|-------------|
| Compute Miner | a GPU | uptime + a cut of insights your compute helped make |
| Prompt Explorer | nothing special | 100 MINE per verified insight + 40% royalties |
| Data Provider | a dataset | 15–20% of every license your data touches |
| Validator | a small stake | steady fees + forfeited stakes of bad actors |
| Model Trainer | a GPU | 10% of licenses that use a model version you improved |
| Curator | a small stake | 3% of licenses you backed early |
| Challenge Miner | a small stake | a share of stakes slashed from false insights |
| Oracle Node | basic hardware | a fee per verification |
| Liquidity Provider | some capital | trading fees + rewards |
| Subnet Operator | a GPU + initiative | 20–30% of your subnet's revenue |

You can run several at once.

## Keyboard shortcuts

- **1–9** — jump to a tab
- **Cmd/Ctrl + K** — command palette (search any action)
- **?** — help
- **Esc** — close

## Cashing out

Open **Cash out**, enter an amount, and withdraw. There's a small fee and it takes about 1–2 business days. You can also send MINE to an external wallet.

## Common questions

**Do I need crypto experience?** No. The app hides all of it.
**Is my computer safe?** You only run the parts you choose, and you can stop anytime.
**What if I have no GPU?** Plenty of roles need none — Explorer, Data Provider, Validator, Curator.
**Is this real money?** In demo mode balances live in the app's database. With a real chain connected, balances mirror on-chain and you can connect a wallet.

See the in-app **FAQ** tab for more.
