SECTION 01 — OPERATING PRINCIPLE

One brain.
Built by everyone.

No jargon. The whole machine runs as a five-step loop. Money flows in at the end and is redistributed to everyone who powered the loop — then a slice is destroyed to make the token scarcer.

01
CONTRIBUTE · COMPUTE

You lend power

Your idle computer joins a global swarm and runs one shard of a giant AI model. No single machine holds the whole brain — it's split across thousands.

02
PRODUCE · INSIGHT

The brain discovers

People feed it sharp questions. It returns genuinely new, checkable ideas — a drug pathway, a battery chemistry, an algorithm — not chit-chat.

03
SECURE · STAKE

It gets verified

Validators stake real money to confirm an idea is original and correct before it's minted as a tradeable "Knowledge Asset."

04
MONETIZE · LICENSE

Someone buys it

A company licenses the verified discovery. Real dollars enter through a smart contract — no middleman, no negotiation.

05
REDISTRIBUTE · BURN

Everyone is paid

The contract auto-splits the payment across every contributor and buys back & burns PROTO — shrinking supply, raising scarcity. Loop repeats.

SECTION 05 — INSIGHT LIFECYCLE · ONE IDEA, END TO END

Prompt → paycheck.

Scroll. This is the exact path of a single discovery — every checkpoint enforced automatically by a smart contract.

STEP 01 · EXPLORE

A prompt is born

Your local explorer-AI writes a sharp question and fires it at the swarm — e.g. "What enzyme breaks down PET plastic at room temperature?"

STEP 02 · INFER

The swarm thinks together

The question is split across many GPUs running model shards. They assemble one answer — a candidate insight — and return it in seconds.

STEP 03 · STAKE + SUBMIT

You back your idea

You stake 10 PROTO and submit on-chain. Skin in the game: garbage submissions cost you money, so the signal stays clean.

STEP 04 · VERIFY

Validators check it's real

Checked for novelty against the global knowledge graph. A validator committee confirms or rejects; challengers can stake against it.

STEP 05 · MINT

It becomes a Knowledge Asset

Approved → you get 100 PROTO + your stake back. The insight is registered on OriginTrail with full provenance: who contributed what.

STEP 06 · LICENSE

A company pays for it

A pharma lab licenses it for real USDC. The contract instantly splits the payment across every contributor — no invoices.

STEP 07 · BURN

The loop closes

The protocol fee buys PROTO on the open market and burns it forever. Supply shrinks, network grows, your tokens gain scarcity.
